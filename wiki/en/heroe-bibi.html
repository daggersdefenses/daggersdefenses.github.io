Chapter 1 (Lv 1) · Show spoiler

The soup

To Bibi, the city was never noisy because of carts or shouting merchants. It was noisy because of the "soup": everyone's thoughts, wishes and fears, all boiling together in her head. She grew up in the alleys believing everyone could hear them. Her real name was lost long ago. She took "Barbara" from the head of a passing noblewoman: it sounded so elegant in there that she kept it. But in such a noisy world, Barbara is too long. Bibi will do.

Chapter 2 (Lv 5) · Show spoiler

Did I think that?

Bibi answers out loud questions a guard only thought. She tells brilliant jokes without knowing whether she made them up or stole them from the innkeeper. That made her the most charming vagabond in the slums: she always knows what to say, because she feels what the other person needs to hear. That's why she prefers small groups. Two heads are a conversation; five are a market.

Chapter 3 (after their quest) · Show spoiler

The ice cube

When the soup boils over, Bibi squeezes an ice cube in her pocket that never melts. "This is my cold, these are my fingers, this is me." In the Glacier she found out where it came from: a piece of Michumi's palace. The queen wanted it back, and for the first time Bibi didn't hide from the voices. She asked them to agree. They did. Since that day she is no longer Bibi of the Thousand Voices: she is Bibi, Who Commands the Voices.

Chapter 4 (Lv 15) · Show spoiler

Magic sleepwalking

By day, Bibi holds the barrier with all her focus. At night it falls. Her body doesn't move, but her magic goes for a walk: whoever sleeps nearby wakes up surrounded by colored lights floating around the camp, made of the dreams she gathered during the day. And then there's the sock. Just one, old, that always teleports exactly thirty feet away from her. Nobody knows why. She has never kept the pair on for long.

Chapter 5 (Lv 20) · Show spoiler

A quiet place

Bibi left the city because, among all the noise, she heard a different note: a distant echo calling her. She followed it down the roads with the ice cube in her pocket, looking for a quiet place for her head. She found it at the Inn. Not that there are fewer voices here: TomTom shouts, Ragnuk sings and Xerth exaggerates. But for the first time, the soup tastes like friends.
