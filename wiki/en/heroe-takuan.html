Takuan

Chapter 1 (Lv 1) · Show spoiler

The four elements

Takuan is a monkey-man raised in a monastery high up a mountain. There he learned to master the four elements: fire, water, earth and air. It took his masters thirty years. It took him six months. The problem is he got bored in the seventh. He leapt down the mountain, literally, and has been in a hurry ever since. Takuan doesn't walk: he arrives. That's why expeditions with him are shorter. There's no time to waste.

Chapter 2 (Lv 5) · Show spoiler

Don't look at me

At the monastery they taught him that focus is sacred. It half worked for Takuan: he can fight with all four elements at once, but if someone stares at him, he loses the thread. His solution was practical: he pays people not to look at him. A merchant, a guard, once an entire gang of bandits. With Elioni they make a perfect team: Takuan pays so nobody looks at them, and Elioni makes sure nobody sees them.

Chapter 3 (after their quest) · Show spoiler

Fire against ice

In the Glacier, Michumi sat waiting on her throne of eternal ice, which nobody had ever melted. Takuan didn't wait for the party to finish planning. He ran, jumped and gave the throne a fire kick. It melted completely. Then, as Michumi glared at him, he paid her not to look at him. It worked. That day he discovered there was a fifth element: patience. He still hasn't mastered it. But since that day he is Takuan, Fist of the Fifth Element.

Chapter 4 (Lv 15) · Show spoiler

The web

Lavek set up the perfect defense: a giant web across the pass, and all they had to do was wait. Takuan waited twelve seconds. Then he got bored, set it on fire with one kick and charged the enemies. Everyone burned, enemies included, and the expedition was a success, though nobody quite knew how. That night at the Inn, Takuan burned a pillow too. He says it was an accident. TomTom no longer gives him feather pillows.

Chapter 5 (Lv 20) · Show spoiler

The fifth element

One morning, the party was slow to leave: Enrique got lost looking for his boots, Vavhal couldn't find his costume and Kupgush was hugging TomTom. Everyone expected Takuan to explode. But Takuan sat down at the Inn's door, closed his eyes and waited. Three whole minutes. Without burning anything. When he opened his eyes, they were all looking at him. He paid them to stop looking. But he was smiling.
