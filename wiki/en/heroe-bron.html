Chapter 1 (Lv 1) · Show spoiler

The White Tower

Bron is a blue-scaled dragonborn, a paladin and captain of the guard of the greatest city in the north. His post is the White Tower, the tallest on the wall. From up there he has watched thieves, armies and once a dragon come and go. None of it scared him. What got him out of the tower was learning that, further south, there were cities where nobody protected anybody. He requested leave. It still hasn't been approved. He left anyway.

Chapter 2 (Lv 5) · Show spoiler

Fines

Bron can't help it: he sees an infraction and pulls out his notebook. He'll fine you for tying your horse badly, for crossing the street without looking or for whistling off-key. Then he'll buy you dinner, because a fine is a fine and dinner is dinner. On his first day at the Inn he fined TomTom for shouting "HOOOO!" above the permitted volume. TomTom paid it, framed it and hung it over the bar.

Chapter 3 (after their quest) · Show spoiler

The White Tower frees the city

Argenen lived under the Governor's laws: no laughing, no singing, fun is mandatory only on the days he says. Bron sent for his guard. The soldiers of the White Tower arrived at dawn, marched down the main avenue and opened the city gates. Bron wrote the first new law himself: "Laughing is allowed every day." Since that day he is Bron, Commander of the White Tower.

Chapter 4 (Lv 15) · Show spoiler

Storm breath

Like every blue dragonborn, Bron breathes lightning. In battle he's fearsome. At the Inn he uses it to toast bread, light candles and, once, to restart TomTom's heart after a fright. Bron fights better the bigger his party: he says a captain without troops is just a man in armor. That's why every companion who joins the expedition gives him a little more strength. And one more fine to review.

Chapter 5 (Lv 20) · Show spoiler

The rulebook

Bron wrote the Inn's rulebook. It has three articles. One: nobody gets left behind. Two: TomTom may shout "HOOOO!" as often as he likes. Three: laughing is allowed every day. He hung it next to his framed fine. His leave from the White Tower still hasn't been approved, and by now he doesn't care. The greatest city in the north has a tower. He has an Inn now, and he guards it just the same.
