Chapter 1 (Lv 1) · Show spoiler

The prince

Ragnuk is a goblin, and also a prince. Of the Traknuk, the northern tribes, and he never lets anyone forget it. He walks with his chin up, talks about himself in the third person when he's happy and demands to be called "Your Highness" at least once a day. He came south to prove a goblin can be as noble as anyone. He's proving it by shouting, which is how he does everything.

Chapter 2 (Lv 5) · Show spoiler

The shop window

In his first week down south, Ragnuk walked into a shop in the mines to buy a cloak fit for a prince. The owner, a dwarf in a pink tunic named Malamar, threw him out for being a goblin. Ragnuk stepped outside, found a rock, weighed it carefully and smashed the window in one throw. Then he left a coin on the counter. "For the glass," he said. "You still owe me the cloak." Malamar has yet to pay up.

Chapter 3 (after their quest) · Show spoiler

The crown nobody gave him

Centuries ago, Ignarok borrowed a crown from a Traknuk king and never gave it back. Ragnuk learned about it from his grandmother's songs. He went to the Ashen Maw with the bill already worked out: the crown, plus centuries of interest. The dragon, who writes down every coin he's owed, had to pay a debt for the first time ever. Ragnuk came back with the crown and so many coins his back hurt. Since that day he is no longer a Prince: he is Ragnuk, King of the Traknuk.

Chapter 4 (Lv 15) · Show spoiler

Xerth's Ballad

Ragnuk is Xerth's number one fan. He knows all his legends by heart, and the ones he doesn't know he makes up. Every night at the Inn he sings Xerth's Ballad at the top of his lungs, in four different keys. Xerth swears it's all true. The only problem is stealth: when he goes with Elioni, Ragnuk announces the party's arrival by shouting and breaks a window. Elioni loses the element of surprise. Ragnuk earns twice the coins. To him it's a good deal.

Chapter 5 (Lv 20) · Show spoiler

The treasurer

With the crown on his head, everyone expected Ragnuk to go back north to rule. He didn't. He says a king must keep watch over his treasure, and his treasure is now at the Inn. He appointed himself treasurer without anyone asking, collects every debt with interest, and that's why the party earns more coins when he's around. TomTom calls him "Your Highness" once a day. Ragnuk forgives his phoenix stew tab. It's a royal arrangement.
