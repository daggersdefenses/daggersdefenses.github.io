The Abyss

It opens when an adventurer reaches Lv 20. Every now and then an Abyss mission shows up on the board (one at a time): 12 hours, 4 adventurers and at most 50% success. Each win gives a Trophy (+1% income forever, up to 10) and turns the heroes and legends who went into Abyss Walkers. With all 15 Walkers, Malgoroth awaits. Also: 2 more rooms for veterans, and legends you dismissed can come back in a rumor.

  • Each new expedition has a 25% chance of being an Abyss mission, if there isn't one on the board or underway.
  • Trophies: +1% income each, up to 10. They survive the Guild.
  • Abyss injuries are grave: 12 h.
  • With the Abyss open, legends you dismissed can return in a rumor, and there are 2 more veteran rooms.

Malgoroth's Raid

All 15 go together, for 24 hours. If they lose, they come back hurt (12 h) and Malgoroth is weaker next time. If they win: the Demonslayer title, a golden frame and +10% income forever. It can only be won once.

  • Requirement: all 15 heroes and legends, at Lv 20, Abyss Walkers, healthy and at the Inn.
  • Chance: 30% on the first try, +5% per failure, up to 50%. It lasts 24 h and there are no combos.
