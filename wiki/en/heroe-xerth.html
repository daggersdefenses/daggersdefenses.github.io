Chapter 1 (Lv 1) · Show spoiler

Green Tongue

Xerth was a pirate captain, or so he says. He has a ship, or had one, or will. They call him "Green Tongue" because of his favorite drink, a seaweed brew that stains his tongue green and his memory every color. The coast where he used to dock bears his nickname: the Green Tongue Coast. He swears it was named in his honor. The fishermen say it's because of the seaweed. Nobody dares argue with him.

Chapter 2 (Lv 5) · Show spoiler

The kraken

Xerth beat a kraken at arm-wrestling. That much is certain. What changes is how. On Tuesdays it was with a spoon. On Thursdays, with tickling. Once it was the kraken who asked for a rematch, and Xerth gave it a head start with one arm tied. Nobody verified it. He doesn't remember either: it was a seaweed-brew night. Still, he tells it with such passion that, when Xerth is around, expeditions get more Resounding Successes. Or at least that's how he tells it.

Chapter 3 (after their quest) · Show spoiler

The verified legend

In the Glacier, before Michumi's palace, the party didn't know what to do. Xerth took out his jar, heated the seaweed brew with a spell and threw it at the wall. The entire palace melted. This time everyone saw it: Takuan, Bibi, Enrique, even Michumi herself. For the first time, one of Xerth's feats had witnesses. Nobody believes him anyway. Since that day he is no longer the Unverifiable: he is Xerth "Green Tongue," the Verified.

Chapter 4 (Lv 15) · Show spoiler

Foul play

Xerth has a line for when things go wrong: "That attack was downright treacherous!" He shouts it when an enemy hits him, when he trips over a root and when TomTom brings the bill. Ragnuk knows all his legends by heart and sings them every night at the Inn, with more verses than Xerth ever made up. Xerth swears it's all true. Lavek, just in case, keeps everything Xerth calls "legendary treasure."

Chapter 5 (Lv 20) · Show spoiler

The last legend

Xerth made a pact with something ancient. He says it was the sea. The sea has never confirmed it. What is certain is that, of all his legends, there is one he tells differently: the one about an Inn with a shouting innkeeper, a hugging orc and a party that always comes back, even when it comes back hurt. That one he tells without exaggerating, slowly, without changing a single detail. It's the only one that is completely true. Nobody believes him.
