FAQ

Do I lose progress if I close the game?

No. Apprentices keep working up to the offline cap and expeditions keep going. Your save is stored locally on your phone.

When should I reset at the Guild?

When your claimable Seals are a good chunk of what you have (say 25–50% more) and production has slowed down.

Why should Crowns and Shields be even?

Because one forge's apprentices are paid with the other's currency, and Seals are based on the weaker one.

I sent a party at 95% and lost.

It happens: 95% is the cap, there's always 5% left. In the Abyss the cap is 50%.

How do I get legends?

By winning Legend Rumors (golden expeditions), which appear after the Coast opens.

What if I dismiss a legend?

They don't come back, except with the Abyss open, where they can reappear in a rumor.

Is the Master's Pass worth it?

It gives you video rewards without watching and ×2 income forever. One-time purchase.
