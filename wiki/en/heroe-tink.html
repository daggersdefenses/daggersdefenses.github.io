Chapter 1 (Lv 1) · Show spoiler

The marbles

Tink Silvabajo is a gnome who counts metal marbles. He has a hundred and twelve. He counts them in the morning, at night and in the middle of battles, in case one got away. Nobody knows what they're for. Neither does Tink, but if one goes missing, you notice: he doesn't sleep, doesn't eat, and searches the whole party's pockets. Once he found it inside the phoenix stew. Kupgush ate it. They had to wait.

Chapter 2 (Lv 5) · Show spoiler

Dino

Dino is a squirrel. They met when Dino stole marble number one hundred. Tink bet he'd get it back before sunset, chased Dino across half a forest and came back with the marble and a squirrel hugging his leg. They've been partners ever since. Tink counts his marbles; Dino counts yours, and then keeps them. If a coin goes missing after talking to Tink, it wasn't Tink. It was Dino. Probably.

Chapter 3 (after their quest) · Show spoiler

Dino, all or nothing

In the Ashen Maw, Ignarok, the Accountant Dragon, charged them a toll for stepping on his ash. Tink offered a bet: if Dino counted the treasure faster than him, they'd pass for free. The dragon laughed. Dino didn't. He counted every coin, stuffed three in his cheeks and finished first. Ignarok is still checking the books. Since that day he is no longer the Squirrel Tamer: he is Tink Silvabajo, Dragon Tamer.

Chapter 4 (Lv 15) · Show spoiler

Double or nothing

With Tink, you never know how much an expedition will pay. On the way back, he always bets the loot: double or nothing. Sometimes they reach the Inn with twice the coins. Sometimes with half, and a goat nobody asked for. TomTom no longer takes his bets, ever since he lost the one about who could eat more phoenix stew: Dino won, and now sleeps on top of the bar, belly up.

Chapter 5 (Lv 20) · Show spoiler

The only bet

Tink has bet everything at some point: coins, boots, a hat that wasn't his and even his horse's name. But there is one thing he won't bet again: Dino. Ignarok was the exception, and afterwards he didn't sleep for a week. He swore never to do it again. Dino swore nothing. And last night, at the Inn, someone saw the squirrel rolling dice with Elioni. Elioni lost the wallet. It was TomTom's.
