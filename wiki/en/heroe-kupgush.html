Kupgush

Hero Warrior

Kupgush the Giant-Hugger

Looks like he'll break your face. He'll hug you instead. Unless you make him angry.

Chapter 1 (Lv 1) · Show spoiler

The hug

Kupgush is a huge orc, with tusks, scars and a braid that reaches his waist. When he walks into a room, people step aside. When he approaches with open arms, people scream. And then they get the best hug of their lives. Kupgush looks like he'll smash your face in, but all he wants is to know how you're doing. TomTom's ribs are still recovering from the first hello.

Chapter 2 (Lv 5) · Show spoiler

The braid

Kupgush's braid holds colored beads. Each one is a friend: a wooden one for Carrlos, a metal one for Slim, an acorn-shaped one Dino picked. The braid gets heavier and heavier, and Kupgush couldn't be happier. There is only one rule: nobody touches it without permission. A bandit tried once. Kupgush got angry, and when Kupgush gets angry, he grows. Literally. The bandit sells flowers now.

Chapter 3 (after their quest) · Show spoiler

The hug that melted the glacier

In the Glacier, everyone feared Michumi, the Ice Queen. Not Kupgush. Kupgush figured that someone who lives alone in an ice palace surely needed a hug. He walked through the snowdrifts, opened his arms and hugged her. The glacier melted from sheer tenderness. Michumi did too, a little. Since that day he is no longer Kupgush the Giant-Hugger: he is Kupgush the Mountain-Hugger.

Chapter 4 (Lv 15) · Show spoiler

Phoenix stew

Every tavern in the world offers phoenix stew. It sounds delicious. It's everything left over from the week, in one pot. Nobody orders it twice. Kupgush orders three bowls, and then seconds. He says you can taste the cook's love. TomTom loves him like a son: he's the only customer who empties the pot. Everyone else orders bread and watches Kupgush eat with a mix of disgust and admiration.

Chapter 5 (Lv 20) · Show spoiler

All together

Kupgush was never the fastest or the cleverest in the party. But when he goes, everyone fights better. It isn't magic: nobody wants to let down the orc who hugged them before leaving. One night at the Inn, Kupgush hugged everyone at once: heroes, legends, TomTom and Dino. Three cracked ribs, and nobody complained. It was the best day of his life. He says that about every day.
