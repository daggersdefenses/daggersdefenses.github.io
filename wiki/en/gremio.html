The Guild

Close both forges and reopen them with the guild's know-how. Each Seal adds +1% to all your earnings, forever.

How many Seals: 150 × √(lifetime total of your weaker forge ÷ 10T). On reset you collect the new Seals earned since the last one. Note: it uses whichever currency you earned less of overall (Crowns or Shields), so keep them even.

Resets

Crowns, Shields, products, apprentices and forge upgrades.

You keep

Seals, Runes, everything bought with Runes, the Inn (adventurers, Amulets and rooms) and your daily gift streak.

Seals can also be spent at the Bastion Smugglers, but spent Seals don't come back on their own: you have to earn them again.
