Chapter 1 (Lv 1) · Show spoiler

The unlucky halfling

Halflings are famous for their luck. Elioni is the exception. If it rained, it rained only on him. If there was a single hole in the road, he found it. If he flipped a coin, it landed in someone else's hand. He learned to live in the shadows because there, at least, bad luck couldn't see him coming. Nobody else could either. In time he became so quiet that sometimes even he didn't know if he had arrived.

Chapter 2 (Lv 5) · Show spoiler

The coin

One day, his best friend got the worst luck of all. Elioni carried him to the temple of the goddess of luck with no hope at all, and saw with his own eyes how his friend breathed again. That night, for the first time in his life, something went right. Since then he carries one of the goddess's coins in his pocket. He spins it between his fingers before every job. He says it isn't superstition. Just in case, he never spends it.

Chapter 3 (after their quest) · Show spoiler

The one who was never there

In Argenen, Elioni walked into the palace, stole the Governor's seal and walked out. Nobody saw him. Not the guards, not the dogs, not the party waiting outside. When he came back with the seal, Vavhal asked if he had gone in yet. Elioni had been back for half an hour. The Governor still doesn't know how it happened. Neither does the party. Did it happen? Since that day he is no longer the Silent Shadow: he is Elioni, the Shadow Nobody Saw (Ever).

Chapter 4 (Lv 15) · Show spoiler

The scar

To become an assassin, Elioni had to pass a trial. He doesn't talk about it. It left a scar on his face that almost nobody has seen, and they say seeing it might be the last thing you do. TomTom swears he saw it once, while serving him phoenix stew. He blinked, Elioni was gone, and so was his wallet. Takuan pays people not to look at them. Elioni doesn't need to pay: people simply can't find him.

Chapter 5 (Lv 20) · Show spoiler

On its edge

The shadows were his refuge, until he teamed up with a goblin who announces their arrival by shouting, a monk who sets everything on fire and a cleric who carries a door. Stealth got complicated. One night at the Inn, he flipped the coin to decide whether to leave. It landed on its edge. Elioni stared at it for a long time, and stayed. He still hides, but now he hides nearby. If you look closely at the darkest corner of the Inn, he's there. No, not there. He's gone.
