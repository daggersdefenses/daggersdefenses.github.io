Daggers & Defenses Wiki

HOOOO! Welcome. Daggers & Defenses is a medieval blacksmith idle game inspired by tabletop RPGs: forge weapons and armor, hire apprentices who work on their own, and use your earnings to run an Inn of adventurers who go on expeditions, level up, complete quests and, someday, descend into the Abyss to face Malgoroth.

Quick start

  1. Tap products to forge. Every unit you buy produces more per cycle.
  2. Hire apprentices: they forge on their own, even with the game closed (up to the offline cap).
  3. At 25, 50, 100, 200, 300 and 400 units of a product, its cycle runs twice as fast.
  4. Buy upgrades: they multiply what a product, or a whole forge, earns.
  5. Keep Crowns and Shields even: one forge's apprentices are paid with the other forge's currency, and Guild Seals are based on the weaker one.
  6. Your 5th product in either forge opens TomTom's Inn.
  7. When production stalls, reset at the Guild: every Seal gives +1% income forever.
