Combos & parties

Some legends together in the same party trigger a combo. The ones marked ⚠️ come with a deliberate cost.

ComboWhoEffect
The Ballad of Xerth
Ragnuk sang the Ballad of Xerth the whole way. It worked.		Ragnuk + Xerth+15% success
⚠️ The web
Lavek built a perfect defense. Takuan set it on fire with one kick. The enemies burned too.		Lavek + Takuan+15% success, but 15% longer
Nobody saw them
Takuan paid everyone not to look. Elioni made sure nobody saw them anyway.		Takuan + ElioniIf they fail, they come back unhurt
Legendary treasure
Xerth said it was a legendary treasure. Lavek kept it, just in case.		Lavek + XerthResounding success = guaranteed Amulet
⚠️ Stealth ruined
Elioni couldn't sneak: Ragnuk announced their arrival by yelling and breaking a window.		Elioni + RagnukElioni loses his bonus, but +30% coins

Xerth's Ballad also works from the Inn: with Ragnuk and Xerth sleeping there, +5% success on every expedition. The Legends' Table: with all 5 legends at the Inn, +10% coins on every expedition.
